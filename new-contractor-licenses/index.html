Questions

How many contractors are in a week's file?

It changes week to week, but it runs to hundreds of new licenses. We fold them to one row per company or person, so a file has fewer rows than licenses. Our Florida counties page shows the latest week's count by county.

Is every new license a new business?

Not always. Some are people licensed under a business that already exists, and a license date can't tell how old a company is. For the newest companies, filter Record type to Company license (QB).

Can I get just my area?

Each file covers all of Florida, and every row has its county and area, so you can filter to yours. Contractors with an out-of-state address are left out. Need something different? Tell me what you need.

Is it OK to email and call these contractors?

How you use the list is up to you. Business email has to follow CAN-SPAM: say who you are, include a working way to opt out and your postal address, and honor opt-outs. Phone numbers are screened against Florida's Do Not Call list, not the National Do Not Call Registry, so check that before you call, and follow Florida and federal telemarketing rules. Each file comes with its terms of use.
