Questions

Where does the data come from?

From the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation's public-records files, which it updates every week. The email addresses come from DBPR's licensing records, which we request under Florida's public records law every week. Nothing is scraped from people-search sites or social media.

Do I need an account or a password?

No. Your desk is a private link that works in any browser, on a computer or a phone. Anyone with the link can open it, so keep it to your agency. If it ever gets out, reply to any LicenseDigest email and I'll switch it off and send you a new one.

Is it OK to email these contractors?

Each ready-made note says who you are, includes your mailing address and gives the contractor an easy way to say no. How you use the list is up to you, including following the federal CAN-SPAM rules for business email. We're not lawyers, so if you're unsure, ask yours.

Can't I download this from the state myself?

You can get most of it. The raw files are public: a 45 MB licensee file and an applicant register of over 100,000 rows with no dates on it. The email addresses aren't in those downloads at all; we request them from DBPR every week. What you're paying for is the weekly comparison that finds what's new, the sorting by territory, the desk, and the checks that run before anything is sent. If you'd rather do that yourself, you should.

How do I change territories or cancel?

Cancel anytime in the customer portal. The link is at the bottom of every report. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you've paid for and isn't pro-rated. To add or change a territory, reply to any report or email support@licensedigest.com, and a person confirms the new total before anything changes.
