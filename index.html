Questions

Is this an x-date list?

Yes. Each row is a contractor's general liability expiration date (the x-date) on file with a Florida county or city, with a phone number and email for most.

Where does the data come from?

From county and city contractor-registration records: the certificates of general liability insurance contractors keep on file there, and the dates they expire. Emails and phone numbers come from the state's licensing records (DBPR), the counties and the cities. Nothing is scraped from people-search sites or social media.

Can I get the carrier and the policy number?

For some contractors, yes. Most lists show only the expiration date, but where a county or city keeps each contractor's certificate of insurance, I can put together a separate file with the insurance carrier, the policy number and the expiration date. It covers a few counties and cities so far, so email me and I'll tell you what I have for your area. The workers' comp dates come from the state's policy records.

Is it OK to email and call these contractors?

How you use the list is up to you, including following the federal CAN-SPAM rules for business email and, for calls, Florida and federal telemarketing rules: calling hours, saying who you are, and no autodialed or prerecorded calls to cell phones without consent. Phone numbers are screened against Florida's Do Not Call list, not the National Do Not Call Registry, so check that before you call. We're not lawyers, so if you're unsure, ask yours.

Do I need an account or a password?

No. The list comes by email every Tuesday, as a spreadsheet and a CSV.

Can't I get this from the counties myself?

Some of it. Each county keeps its own records: a few let you search them online, and others send them only on a public records request. We collect them, match each contractor to an email and a phone number from the state's licensing records or the county, screen the phones against Florida's Do Not Call list, and sort it all by area every Tuesday. If you'd rather do that yourself, you should.

How do I change my area or cancel?

Plans renew automatically at the end of each period until you cancel. A one-time file or a custom invoice doesn't renew unless we've agreed that with you first. Cancel anytime in your billing portal, or email support@licensedigest.com. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you've paid for and isn't pro-rated. To change your area, reply to any LicenseDigest email or write to support@licensedigest.com, and a person confirms the new total before anything changes.
