What's on every row

  • The business and the contact person, as the county has them.
  • Trade, city and county.
  • The general liability expiration date on file with the county, and how many days out it is.
  • The workers' comp expiration date, when the county has one on file.
  • An email address and a phone number, from the state's licensing records or the county, with the phone checked against Florida's Do Not Call list. Numbers on that list are left out.
  • The state license number, which county's records the row came from, and the date of that county's file.

A contractor may have renewed or changed carriers since the county's file was made. Confirm the date before you quote.

Questions

Where does the data come from?

From county contractor-registration records: the certificates of general liability insurance contractors keep on file with the county, and the dates they expire. Emails and phone numbers come from the state's licensing records (DBPR) and the counties. Nothing is scraped from people-search sites or social media.

Can I get the carrier and the policy number?

Not from the county lists, which show the expiration date only (Citrus County also names the carrier). The workers' comp dates come from the state's policy records.

Is it OK to email and call these contractors?

The desk's ready-made notes say who you are, include your mailing address and give the contractor an easy way to say no. How you use the list is up to you, including following the federal CAN-SPAM rules for business email and, for calls, Florida and federal telemarketing rules: calling hours, saying who you are, and no autodialed or prerecorded calls to cell phones without consent. Phone numbers are screened against Florida's Do Not Call list, not the National Do Not Call Registry, so check that before you call. We're not lawyers, so if you're unsure, ask yours.

Do I need an account or a password?

No. The list comes by email every Tuesday, as a spreadsheet and a CSV. On region and all-of-Florida plans, your desk is a private link that works in any browser, on a computer or a phone. Anyone with the link can open it, so keep it to your agency. If it ever gets out, reply to any LicenseDigest email and I'll switch it off and send you a new one.

Can't I get this from the counties myself?

Some of it. Each county keeps its own records: a few let you search them online, and others send them only on a public records request. We collect them, match each contractor to an email and a phone number from the state's licensing records or the county, screen the phones against Florida's Do Not Call list, and sort it all by area every Tuesday. If you'd rather do that yourself, you should.

How do I change my area or cancel?

Cancel anytime in your billing portal, or email support@licensedigest.com. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you've paid for and isn't pro-rated. To change your area, reply to any LicenseDigest email or write to support@licensedigest.com, and a person confirms the new total before anything changes.
