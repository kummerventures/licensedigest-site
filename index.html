Questions

Where does the data come from?

From the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation's public-records files, which it updates every week. Nothing is scraped from people-search sites or social media.

Can't I download this from the state myself?

You can. The raw files are public: a 45 MB licensee file and an applicant register of over 100,000 rows with no dates on it. What you're paying for is the weekly comparison that finds what's new, the sorting by territory, and the checks that run before anything is sent. If you'd rather do that yourself, you should.

How do I change territories or cancel?

Cancel anytime in the customer portal. The link is at the bottom of every report. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you've paid for and isn't pro-rated. To add or change a territory, reply to any report or email support@licensedigest.com, and a person confirms the new monthly total before anything changes.
