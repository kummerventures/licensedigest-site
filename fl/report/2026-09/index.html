Where these numbers come from

These are counts from Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR) public licensing records. A new license or new company is counted in the month DBPR dates it. An application has no state date, so it is counted in the month it was first seen in DBPR's applicant file. Each record is counted once, even when it shows up in more than one weekly file. A county is where the contractor's address on file is. Counts run through our Oct 6, 2026 file.

Out-of-state and unknown addresses are counted in the statewide numbers and in the trade table, and left out of the territory and county tables.
